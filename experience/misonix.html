Overview

Misonix builds ultrasonic surgical systems. I supported two product lines across three internship terms, working in the lab, on design changes, and on regulatory documentation.

What I did

  • Ran verification and validation testing and data collection, including 510(k) submission testing, for Class II ultrasonic bone-cutting devices.
  • Engineered a tube set that delivers cooling and aspiration to the BoneScalpel Access handpiece inside the sterile field. I drafted the print and executed the engineering change order.
  • Authored Bioventus's first sterile-field Instructions for Use, working with regulatory affairs, vendors, and a graphic designer to confirm compliance and autoclave compatibility.
  • Drove an ECO for BoneScalpel Access disposable packaging, releasing updated prints and auditing every vendor submission against spec.
  • Served as lab technician for BoneScalpel Access bench work and wrote a report for every test. I also owned the product's test-data organization.
  • Ran daily supply-chain meetings and vendor follow-ups to hold prototype and production deadlines.

Test methods

LabVIEW data acquisition, signal analyzers, thermal imaging, resonance testing, thermocouples, optical amplitude analysis, and operational testing.
