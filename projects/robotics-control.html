Manipulator Dynamics & Sliding-Mode Control

Deriving robot-arm dynamics and comparing controllers on the same plant, ending with a robust sliding-mode design.

  • WhenSpring 2026
  • Team3 engineers
  • CourseEE 5325 Robotics (graduate)
  • ToolsMATLAB · Simulink

Overview

Across a sequence of problem sets we derived and simulated manipulator dynamics, then tested different control strategies against the same robot model to see where each one breaks.

What we did

  • Derived the manipulator equations of motion and simulated them in MATLAB/Simulink.
  • Compared several controller formulations on the same plant. One demanded an unrealistic 2000 N·m of torque, which motivated a more robust approach.
  • Implemented sliding-mode control: derived the sliding surface, virtual reference, and torque law, so tracking error converges despite model uncertainty.
  • Compared results across strategies and presented the findings.
The header figure is illustrative. It shows trajectories from many initial states sliding onto a surface (orange) and converging to zero error, which is the core idea behind sliding-mode control.
