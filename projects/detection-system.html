Motorized Detection System

A rotating ultrasonic sensor platform that sweeps for objects and stops when something comes within 10 cm.

  • WhenSpring 2025
  • GroupStepper Motor Group
  • CourseEE 2240 · UT Arlington
  • ToolsArduino · Multisim

Overview

A fully enclosed unit that works with no setup. It rotates an ultrasonic sensor continuously with a green status LED, then halts and switches to red when an object comes within 10 cm. A relay is enabled or disabled by a partner team's system over SPI.

What I did

  • Designed the control logic for rotation, thresholding, LED status, and SPI-gated relay control.
  • Simulated the full circuit in Multisim, including a power budget for the Arduino, stepper, sensor, and relay.
  • Prototyped on a breadboard and captured PWM, trigger/echo, and relay waveforms on the oscilloscope.
  • Designed a PCB integrating a buck converter, relay, shunt voltage sensor, motor driver, and RGB indicator, with separated power and ground rails.
  • Verified every connection with a multimeter and ran a 5-minute burn-in on the final assembly.
